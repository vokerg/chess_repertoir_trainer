## Accepted decision

The user accepted the RB-021 architecture on 2026-08-01.

The project will implement opening descriptions and strategic plans as a separate, deterministic, source-controlled and independently versioned `OpeningKnowledgeService` beside the existing opening lookup and classification services.

The accepted foundation is:

- consume the resolved `OpeningBookEntry` and existing `OpeningClassificationResult`;
- primarily select knowledge through stable classification rule IDs;
- permit narrow name/ECO/UCI selectors only for strategic distinctions that do not belong in classification;
- apply explicit broad-family, subfamily and line ordering;
- keep descriptions and independent White/Black strategic plans outside `OpeningSideClassification`;
- use stable plan IDs with deterministic merge, explicit removal and complete side replacement;
- preserve conditions, caveats, confidence, lifecycle, source IDs and matched-rule provenance;
- expose only `REVIEWED`, project-original prose in normal runtime results;
- keep unavailable and partial knowledge explicit;
- require no database, background job, runtime LLM or runtime web lookup.

## Authority boundaries

- Opening classification remains the compact intrinsic taxonomy.
- Opening knowledge remains explanatory and has separate versioning and coverage.
- RB-023 may display knowledge but cannot change deterministic candidate ranking, eligibility, fit, coverage, Builder state or course writes.
- RB-024 remains an optional, on-demand, non-authoritative AI game-review consumer.

## Source policy accepted

- `lichess-org/chess-openings` remains the CC0 identity source for ECO, names, moves and positions.
- Public opening pages, studies, articles, videos and books are reference-only unless exact reuse rights are verified.
- Direct CC BY-SA adaptation is not the default workflow.
- AI may assist research and drafting, but runtime text must be original, source-bound, cross-checked and editorially reviewed.
