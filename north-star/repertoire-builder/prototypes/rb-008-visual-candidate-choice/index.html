Direction A

Board-first decision desk

One large board remains the spatial anchor. Candidate buttons update the same position preview; evidence and opponent coverage live in a focused side stack.

  • Optimizes board readability and deep preview.
  • Reuses the current two-column workbench mental model.
  • Requires quick candidate switching to compare structures.
Best for

Desktop workbench, keyboard use, deeper line exploration.

Risk

Evidence can feel secondary or require memory between candidates.

Direction B

Candidate landscape

Three resulting positions are visible at once. Each candidate is a self-contained visual card, followed by a branch coverage matrix.

  • Optimizes direct comparison and tradeoff scanning.
  • Makes the relationship between move and structure explicit.
  • Uses more screen area and risks card overload.
Best for

Initial selection, visual comparison, transparent evidence.

Risk

Mini-boards can become too small; mobile needs horizontal paging.
